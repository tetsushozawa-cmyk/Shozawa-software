もちろん、もう充分日本語を勉強した。と言う方も歓迎です。ここのページは少しだけ、

日本語を斜に構えてみています。なぜなら私も日本語が上手じゃないから、

------

First of all, this page is for people who are just about to start learning Japanese,

people who have already started but still find it confusing, and people who once gave up but somehow still can't stop thinking about it.

Of course, if you've already studied plenty of Japanese, you're more than welcome too.

On this page, we're going to look at Japanese from a slightly different angle.

Why?

Because I'm not very good at Japanese either.

And I've been speaking it for seventy years!

So, let's begin.

------

How to Use This Course

This course is not based on quizzes.

Learning happens in a simple cycle:

Learn → Use → Remember → Use Again

Every time you use Japanese,

remembering becomes easier.
